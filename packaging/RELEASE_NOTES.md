**Orbit 0.2.1**: small update to 0.2.0.

- The apps' texts now match the new Orbit: no more "double-press the dial" when you use earbuds, keys or a knob. The tray, notification, home screen and Android's accessibility description describe your own Open menu button.
- Android "Wheel won't open?" help covers any earbuds (with the CMF Buds Pro 2 steps as one case).
- About shows "by Yemcube (@yem.cube)". The license now asks for credit when you share something built on Orbit publicly ([LICENSE](https://github.com/harsha20599/orbit/blob/main/LICENSE)).
- New step-by-step guide in the [README](https://github.com/harsha20599/orbit#readme).

Everything from 0.2.0 is unchanged: control Orbit with anything, simple setup, camera video on Android, more reliable buttons.

**Questions, problems or ideas?** Join **[Orbit on Telegram](https://t.me/orbit_issues)**.

**Downloads** (Assets below): Windows `Orbit-Windows-Setup.exe` · macOS `Orbit-macOS.zip` · Android `Orbit-Android.apk`. Install over your current version; settings are kept. macOS: after updating, switch Orbit off and on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
